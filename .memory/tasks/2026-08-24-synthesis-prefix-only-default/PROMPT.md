## Objective
Make pre60 accepted-parent Far/Near approach-prefix synthesis without retreat the default production operation and merge it to `dev` after Server1 validation.

## Contract
- Both modes consume the canonical pre60 bundle.
- Far samples its established distant start.
- Near may use accepted-parent movement-end+15 only to sample its retreat-like approach start.
- Both prepend the established 4 cm approach prefix and then preserve the complete original reference tail.
- No retreat suffix is generated, appended, or substituted; policy remains active after the prefix through the original tail.
- Prefix collision rejection remains active.
- The three-predicate save gate (reason 1, final XYZ Euler mean <=35°, at least 101 right-hand/target-object >0.2 N frames) applies to prefix-only candidates.
- Manifest and augmentation identity must record `retreat=null` under a new default prefix-only production contract.
- Historical retreat code/data remain identifiable and are not rewritten.

## Deliverables
1. Exporter contract and acceptance semantics.
2. Default single-job and all-object Far/Near prefix-only entrypoints.
3. Tests and user-facing documentation.
4. Server1 Far and Near GPU smoke using exact committed sources.
5. Feature merge into `dev` without touching primary-worktree user files.
