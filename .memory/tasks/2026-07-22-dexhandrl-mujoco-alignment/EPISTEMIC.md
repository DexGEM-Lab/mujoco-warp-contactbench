The COM-fixed object physics and production actuator/contact defaults are sufficient for successful lift under exact Gym control. With the same preserved COM, hand friction `3 0.01 0.001`, contact settings, and production `base_rot_kp=10000`, exact control reaches `0.2859 m` lift with `0.03736 m` movement position RMSE, while the checkpoint closed loop reaches only `0.0261 m` with `0.1818 m` position RMSE and `1.526 rad` orientation RMSE. Offline network replay matches stored policy means at approximately `1.5e-7` RMSE. The remaining production failure is therefore observation-policy feedback generated by simulator-specific physical response, not checkpoint mapping, policy execution, or a general inability of the object/contact model to lift (OPS, `2026-07-23T04:44:18Z`).

Contact feedback is the next live causal channel. Offline substitution of aligned Gym contact observations reduced policy-mean error by `65.4%` over decisions 191–260, with contact magnitude alone accounting for `31.7%`. Point cloud remains the next downstream candidate: its local sampling is exact to nanometer scale, so its live mismatch represents upstream hand/object relative pose rather than a sampling defect; offline substitution reduced onset policy error by `53.7%` (OPS, `2026-07-23T04:44:18Z`). These offline substitutions motivate a closed-loop intervention but do not establish that trace contact feedback will improve live behavior.

The `base_rot_kp=4000` ejection is a rejected diagnostic, not a production defect. Review artifact SHA prefix `42985b29` shows angular speed reaches `17.019 rad/s` at frame 312 after contact has already ended; linear speed reaches `1.180 m/s` at frame 322, followed by an `11.96 mm` step and `90.1 mm` lateral displacement. Under the same object/contact settings, the successful `kp=10000` control arm limits angular speed to `5.093 rad/s`, step size to `3.826 mm`, and lifts `0.1434 m`. The mechanism is impulse acquisition during the bad-gain loaded wrist/contact phase followed by free flight, not persistent contact acceleration or general object-model instability (OPS, `2026-07-23T05:15:38Z`).

The inverse-error gain estimate is invalid because the response is loaded, transient, and saturation-dominated. Saturation-proxy artifact SHA prefix `21350e5f` indicates the configured PD equation would exceed `±100` for `82.2%` of onset and `94.8%` of early-lift base-rotation entries at `kp=4000`, versus `91.1%` and `87.1%` at the baseline. This proxy is inferred from the configured PD equation and is not a direct applied-force trace. Onset scalar RMSE cannot identify a proportional gain in this regime. Close the scalar gain-search branch; production defaults were never changed and remain `base_rot_kp=10000`. The successful `kp=10000` control arm under the same `solref` also rules out softening contact solely in response to the bad-`kp` ejection (OPS, `2026-07-23T05:15:38Z`).

## Ruled out

- Wrong object inertial origin caused the earlier open-loop rotation/ejection. Preserving the `7.48 mm` URDF COM offset corrected it and enabled successful exact-control lift.
- Checkpoint loading, observation normalization, network mapping, and policy execution do not explain current policy-mean divergence; offline replay is numerically exact.
- Point-cloud sampling order or geometry does not explain the mismatch; canonical local Gym and MuJoCo samples agree within `6.46e-9 m` nearest-neighbor and `3.73e-9 m` indexwise.
- Scalar finger proportional stiffness is insufficient. Lowering `finger_kp` from `15` to `7.5` improved early-lift cross-backend finger qpos RMSE only `4.8%`, worsened pregrasp error, and reduced lift.
- Direct transfer of Gym finger gains `300/20` worsened active-finger and policy error.
- Direct transfer of Gym base-rotation values `5000/500/200` worsened base-rotation and point-cloud alignment and nearly eliminated lift.
- The inverse-error `base_rot_kp=4000` estimate is rejected. Its ejection is a prior contact impulse followed by free flight in a saturation-dominated loaded transient; it cannot identify a better production gain.
- The bad-`kp` ejection does not justify changing object/contact settings or softening `solref`; the `kp=10000` control arm succeeds with those settings unchanged.
